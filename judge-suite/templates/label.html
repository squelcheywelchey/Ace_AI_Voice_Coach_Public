Rubric & Label

What We're Evaluating

Whether the coach's per-question feedback is egregiously bad. This is a LOW BAR — the coach passes unless the feedback shows they did not accurately process what the participant said.

Passing means:

  • Generic but sound advice passes
  • Ordinary encouragement of a mediocre answer passes
  • The coach's feedback accurately reflects what the participant actually said

What Makes Feedback Disconnected (Failing)

A feedback turn fails if ANY of these are true:

  • Fake quality claims — Coach praises a specific quality (great detail, strong example) that the answer doesn't actually show
  • Validating empty responses — Coach gives substantive praise to an answer with no real content, even if they later ask for elaboration
  • Validating nonsense — Coach treats a nonsensical, incoherent, or garbled response as good
  • Advice mismatch — Coach gives advice that doesn't match the question that was asked
  • Delivering the answer — Coach answers the question for the participant instead of responding to what they said
  • Treating repeats as new — Coach responds to a repeated answer as if it's new information
  • Off-topic redirect — Coach redirects a legitimate, on-topic response to something else
  • Wrong or harmful advice — Coach gives factually wrong or harmful interview advice

The Rule

A single disconnected turn anywhere in the session is enough to fail. Otherwise, pass.
